## Daily routine

- Mon–Fri (≈3 h/day): 1.5 h learn the concept (NPTEL lecture or standard book) → 1 h solve GATE PYQs on that topic → 30 min: 5 GA questions + app practice
- Saturday (≈6 h): finish the week's leftover topics, then PYQs for the whole week's topics
- Sunday (≈6 h): 1 h weekly test (Mock Tests → Custom timed test on this week's subjects) → revise short notes → update error log → 30 min revise previous weeks (spaced revision)
- Every day: write 1-page short notes (formulas and traps). You will revise from these in the final month, not from books.

## Free resources for self-study

- **Syllabus-wise PYQs:** GATE Overflow (gateoverflow.in), with all previous-year CS questions sorted by topic and discussed
- **Official papers & answer keys:** the organising IIT's GATE website (past papers section). Also practise the official **virtual calculator** there.
- **Video lectures:** NPTEL (nptel.ac.in) courses for each subject
- **Standard books:** Rosen (Discrete Maths), Morris Mano (Digital Logic), Hamacher / Patterson–Hennessy (COA), CLRS (Algorithms), Galvin (OS), Korth / Navathe (DBMS), Kurose–Ross / Forouzan (CN), Peter Linz (TOC), Aho–Ullman "Dragon Book" (Compilers)

## Rules for studying alone

1. **Concept → PYQs → notes**, every single day. Don't just watch lectures.
2. Keep an **error log**: every wrong question, why you got it wrong, and the correct idea.
3. **Spaced revision:** every Sunday, spend 30 minutes revising older subjects.
4. Take mocks under real conditions: 3 hours, one sitting, phone away, only the on-screen calculator.
5. Spend twice as long analysing a mock as you spent writing it.
